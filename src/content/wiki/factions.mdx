## What faction choice changes

Faction assignment determines your side in the three-team match: which players count as friendly, which color identity you read on the battlefield and which team contributes presence toward the Control Zone.

The official Supporter Pack also confirms faction-branded weapon cosmetics for all three teams, which establishes cosmetic identity without proving separate combat balance.

| Possible difference | Verified? |
|---|---|

| Exclusive weapon stat bonuses | No |

Community discussion contains lore interpretations and stereotypes about team strength, but those should not be converted into hard mechanics without official patch documentation.

## Why three teams change the match

A three-team objective creates situations a two-sided shooter does not. Two factions can be fighting while the third takes space elsewhere, or one team can become the focus of both opponents. That means faction awareness is primarily a **map-reading and threat-direction problem** rather than a build-selection bonus.
