## Build anatomy

<div class="wiki-data-split">
<div>
### Core slots
| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Primary | What range and task am I buying for? |
| Protection | How much survivability is worth the cost? |
| Storage | How much utility must I carry? |
| Specialist | Does the role require a launcher, medical or support tool? |
| Vehicle | Is mobility part of this life’s plan? |
</div>
<div>
### Three questions before checkout
1. What is my job this life?
2. How much am I willing to lose?
3. What must still work if I survive the first fight?

A sustainable loadout is good enough for the task and cheap enough to rebuild.
</div>
</div>

## Loadout reference matrix

| Role | Prioritize | Common waste |
|---|---|---|
| General infantry | Reliable primary, protection, simple utility | Over-specializing before reading the route |
| Medic | Survival, revive access, manageable weapon cost | Spending so much that every death breaks the bankroll |
| Pilot | Vehicle access, light practical kit, transport plan | Buying frontline assault gear that rarely gets used |
| Support / logistics | Capacity, tools, repeatability | Paying for combat gear unrelated to the support task |
