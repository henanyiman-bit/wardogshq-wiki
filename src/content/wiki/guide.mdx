<div class="ref-check-grid">
<div class="ref-check"><strong>Control Zone wins matches</strong><span>The three teams compete for presence in a randomized 2×2 km scoring area.</span></div>
<div class="ref-check"><strong>Cash survives the match</strong><span>Your purchases are part of a longer economy, not a disposable round budget.</span></div>
<div class="ref-check"><strong>Support actions pay</strong><span>Reviving, transporting friendlies and objective play are all valid economic contributions.</span></div>
