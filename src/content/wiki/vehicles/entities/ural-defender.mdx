## Battlefield Role

The Ural Defender is the protected step above the base logistics truck. Its $6,000 observed cost should be justified by routes where exposure is likely but a dedicated weapon station is unnecessary. It remains a supply vehicle: protection helps a run survive contact, but it does not turn the crew into a frontline assault unit.

## Mobility

With the same observed 79 speed and 85 fuel reference as the base Ural, route planning remains deliberate. Favor covered approaches, use terrain to mask the final leg and keep enough space to leave without a complicated turn. The extra purchase cost is wasted if the driver repeats the same exposed path after contact.

## Crew Usage

Three seats support a driver, navigator and logistics specialist. The navigator should track alternate roads and warn when the current destination becomes too active. On arrival, unload or complete the support task quickly; remaining stationary near a FOB or objective makes the vehicle easier to locate and removes its mobility advantage.

## Strengths

- Defensive variant for riskier supply routes.
- Strong fuel reference for sustained logistics work.
- Costs less than the weapon-equipped Defender M249.

## Weaknesses

- $1,000 more than the standard Ural.
- Identical seat count and speed do not improve throughput.
- Protection does not guarantee survival against concentrated threats.
