## Tactical role

UH-1Y belongs to the **fast squad transport** part of the vehicle roster. It is most relevant to **moving useful players across long distances and bypassing ground routes**. That mission framing is more useful than choosing a vehicle from silhouette alone, because WARDOGS makes every loss a cash decision.

## Economy check

Against the $10,000 starting balance, one observed purchase leaves **$2,600** before any personal loadout spending.

One loss also creates replacement exposure. Before leaving the vendor, decide whether the trip, transport capacity or fire-support role is valuable enough to justify buying the platform again if the first attempt fails.
