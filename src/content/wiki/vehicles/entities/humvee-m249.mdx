## Battlefield Role

The Humvee M249 is a five-seat transport that adds a light support weapon to squad movement. Its observed $3,750 price is best justified when the gunner can protect a road crossing, cover a dismount or discourage infantry pressure. It should carry players to a task rather than circle the objective looking for targets.

## Mobility

The 113.2 speed reference supports quick relocation between cover points. Plan short pauses where the gunner has a useful arc, then move before opponents can concentrate on the vehicle. A 65 fuel reference means the crew should combine transport and security goals in one route instead of making unnecessary patrol laps.

## Crew Usage

Use a dedicated driver and gunner, leaving three seats for the team being transported. The gunner should communicate threats early and stop firing when the driver needs concealment. Passengers should dismount before the vehicle commits to a firing position so one hit does not remove the entire group.

## Strengths

- Five-seat capacity combines crew and passenger roles.
- M249 configuration adds useful infantry suppression context.
- Moderate premium over the base Humvee.

## Weaknesses

- Requires coordination between driver and gunner.
- Firing reveals the transport's position.
- More expensive than an unarmed delivery vehicle.
