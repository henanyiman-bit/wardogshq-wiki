## Battlefield Role

The Ural Defender M249 combines a logistics mission with an armed security option. At an observed $6,750, it should be chosen when a supply crew expects harassment along the route and has a passenger available to operate the weapon. Buying it for an uncontested run adds cost without creating meaningful extra value.

## Mobility

Its 79 speed and 85 fuel reference match the other Ural variants, so the mounted weapon should not encourage reckless approaches. Maintain distance from the frontline, stop where the gunner can watch likely threats and preserve an exit route. The safest logistics run is still one that avoids contact entirely.

## Crew Usage

The three seats demand clear role assignment: driver, gunner and logistics operator. The gunner protects movement and withdrawal rather than searching for fights. During unloading, the driver should remain ready to move while the third player handles the support task, reducing the time the vehicle stays exposed.

## Strengths

- Adds an armed response to the Ural logistics platform.
- High fuel reference supports repeat routes.
- Useful for supply corridors with recurring infantry pressure.

## Weaknesses

- Highest observed price among the three Ural variants.
- Only three seats, so the weapon role consumes a large share of the crew.
- A gunner can distract the team from the actual logistics objective.
