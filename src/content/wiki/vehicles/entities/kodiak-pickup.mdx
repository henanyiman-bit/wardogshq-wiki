## Battlefield Role

The Kodiak Pickup emphasizes passenger capacity over a mounted weapon. Six observed seats make it the practical choice for moving a larger infantry group quickly while keeping every passenger available after dismount. At $3,000, it costs more than the base Kodiak but less than armed variants that dedicate crew attention to a gun.

## Mobility

The 154 speed reference supports rapid team movement, yet a full passenger load increases the consequence of a bad route. Use covered staging points, call the dismount early and avoid stopping where all six occupants face the same threat. Its 50 fuel reference favors direct deliveries and planned return routes.

## Crew Usage

One driver can move five passengers, giving the Pickup excellent crew efficiency. Assign a passenger to navigation and rear observation during travel. At the destination, spread the dismount instead of clustering around the vehicle, then decide whether the driver joins the fight or preserves transport for the next movement.

## Strengths

- Six seats provide the best capacity in this light-transport comparison.
- High observed speed supports rapid reinforcement.
- No dedicated gunner means more players arrive ready to dismount.

## Weaknesses

- A full vehicle concentrates risk in one route decision.
- No mounted weapon is established for this configuration.
- Low fuel reference limits repeated detours and patrol behavior.
