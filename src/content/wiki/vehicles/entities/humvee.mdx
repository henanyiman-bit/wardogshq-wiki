## Battlefield Role

The base Humvee is a four-seat road transport for moving a compact squad without paying for a mounted weapon. Its observed $3,000 price fits repeatable repositioning, reinforcement and quick objective approaches. The vehicle is most efficient when all four seats contribute after dismount rather than keeping a passenger tied to a gun station.

## Mobility

An observed speed of 113.2 gives the Humvee enough pace to change routes when a road becomes unsafe. Its fuel reference of 65 is lower than the Ural's, so drivers should avoid unnecessary loops and abandoned long detours. Slow before blind intersections and choose a dismount point that does not expose every passenger at once.

## Crew Usage

One driver and three passengers make a natural small-squad transport. The passengers should watch different sectors during movement and dismount on the protected side. After delivery, decide whether the driver will hide the vehicle for extraction or return for another group rather than leaving it visible beside the objective.

## Strengths

- Four useful transport seats at a moderate observed price.
- Faster reference speed than logistics trucks.
- No dedicated gunner requirement.

## Weaknesses

- No mounted weapon on the base configuration.
- Lower fuel reference rewards disciplined routing.
- A full squad can be lost if the driver chooses an exposed approach.
