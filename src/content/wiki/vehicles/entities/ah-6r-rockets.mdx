## Tactical role

AH-6R Rockets belongs to the **fast airborne fire support** part of the vehicle roster. It is most relevant to **rapid response and vertical access with high loss risk**. That mission framing is more useful than choosing a vehicle from silhouette alone, because WARDOGS makes every loss a cash decision.

## Economy check

The observed purchase is **$2,500 above** the $10,000 starting balance, so the platform assumes prior earnings or saved cash.

One loss also creates replacement exposure. Before leaving the vendor, decide whether the trip, transport capacity or fire-support role is valuable enough to justify buying the platform again if the first attempt fails.
