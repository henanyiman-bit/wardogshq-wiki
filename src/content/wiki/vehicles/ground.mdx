## Use the vehicle for a team job

WARDOGS explicitly rewards teamplay and transport. A vehicle is therefore not only a weapon platform; mobility, supply movement and getting friendlies to the objective can be part of its economic value.

## Ground-vehicle decision guide

<div class="quick-grid">
<div class="quick-card"><strong>Transport first</strong><span>Can this move useful players or supplies where they need to go?</span></div>
<div class="quick-card"><strong>Firepower second</strong><span>A stronger gun is only valuable if the vehicle can survive and influence the fight.</span></div>
<div class="quick-card"><strong>Cost awareness</strong><span>Persistent cash means losing a heavy platform hurts future options.</span></div>
<div class="quick-card"><strong>Map fit</strong><span>Open approaches, streets and objective distance all change the right choice.</span></div>
</div>

<div class="subpage-decision-grid">
<div><strong>Before you buy</strong><span>Define the task and the route before spending persistent cash.</span></div>
<div><strong>During the life</strong><span>Judge the kit or vehicle by whether it performs that task repeatedly.</span></div>
<div><strong>After a loss</strong><span>Review whether the purchase is still sustainable from the remaining balance.</span></div>
</div>
