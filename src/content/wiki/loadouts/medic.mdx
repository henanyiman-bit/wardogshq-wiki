## Medic loadout priorities

| Priority | What to solve | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revive access | The role cannot function without the tools needed to restore teammates |
| 2 | Survival | A dead Medic cannot finish the revive or support the next casualty |
| 3 | Mobility | Reaching the downed player safely matters as much as the revive interaction |
| 4 | Sustainable weapon | Enough combat ability to defend the rescue without exhausting the budget |

## Approach the casualty before committing

A revive is valuable only if you can complete it. Use cover, read the direction of incoming fire and avoid sprinting directly into the same angle that downed the teammate. The goal is not simply to interact with the body; it is to restore a player **and keep both players alive**.

## Revives and the economy

Team17 explicitly lists reviving squadmates as a cash-rewarded action. That makes Medic one of the clearest examples of the game rewarding non-kill contribution. The persistent economy also means a sustainable Medic life can generate value repeatedly instead of betting everything on one expensive kit.

## Medic progression

Season 1 includes a Medic progression path. Unlock relationships can change during Early Access, so this page focuses on the role logic while [Progression](/progression/) owns the versioned unlock structure.

## Medic checklist

1. Stay near useful friendly pressure.
2. Carry the medical access required for the intended revive task.
3. Use enough protection to survive the rescue, not so much that the kit becomes disposable luxury.
4. Clear or reduce the immediate threat before exposing yourself.
5. Keep the revived player connected to the objective flow.
6. Re-check current medical item descriptions after patches.
