There is no defensible single “best loadout” for every WARDOGS life. The game’s persistent cash system means the right kit changes with **role, route, objective distance, expected fight and replacement cost**.

> **Scope note:** exact item prices and balance values can change during Early Access. This page compares build structure and role requirements; it does not turn incomplete public stats into a permanent tier list.

## Role-based loadout matrix

| Role | Primary need | Spend first on | Avoid overspending on |
|---|---|---|---|
| General infantry | Reliable combat across expected range | Primary, ammo, basic protection | Specialist gear with no task |
| Medic | Reach and survive revive opportunities | Medical access, mobility, survivability | Heavy combat extras that reduce repeatability |
| Support / builder | Carry tools and supplies | Storage, role tools, practical defense | Expensive gun upgrades unrelated to the build task |
| Driver | Reach and operate ground vehicles | Vehicle access, practical backup kit | Infantry luxury that competes with vehicle cash |
| Pilot | Reach and operate aircraft | Aircraft access, traversal, light backup gear | Maximum-cost rifleman kit |

## Four things every strong build has to solve

<div class="wiki-callout-grid">
<div><strong>Task</strong><span>What are you doing this life: hold, revive, transport, build or fight at range?</span></div>
<div><strong>Route</strong><span>Will you cross open terrain, enter buildings, ride in a vehicle or stay near a forward position?</span></div>
<div><strong>Budget</strong><span>Can the kit be replaced after an ordinary death without destroying future options?</span></div>
</div>

## General infantry

A flexible infantry build should cover the likely engagement range, carry enough ammunition and medical support for more than one contact, and leave enough cash for another life. It does not need every available attachment or the most expensive protection tier to be useful.

## Medic

Medic value comes from reaching downed teammates and surviving long enough to create several support opportunities. The [Medic Loadout](/loadouts/medic/) page focuses on revive-oriented priorities rather than trying to make the Medic a maximum-damage rifleman.

## Driver and Pilot

Vehicle roles are unusual because a large portion of the life’s value may be purchased **outside the infantry kit**. A cheaper practical weapon can be the correct choice when the main investment is a truck, armored vehicle or helicopter.

## Support and logistics

Support builds should treat storage and task tools as core equipment. The expensive part of a useful support life may be the construction or logistics function rather than the firearm.

## When a loadout is actually “best”

A build deserves that label only when it fits the current task, can reach the relevant part of the map, and can be replaced at a reasonable cost. That makes “best” conditional rather than universal.
