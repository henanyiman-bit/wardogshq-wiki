## What the developer says Early Access is for

The Steam Early Access statement says the goal is to put a fully playable FPS in players' hands early, gather meaningful feedback and iterate on **balance, pacing, progression and features** alongside the community. Steam also says the expected Early Access period is roughly **1–2 years**, while noting that plans can change during development.

## Why version awareness matters

A guide can stay useful even when one exact number becomes old. For example, the task flow for flying transport can remain useful while a helicopter price changes. The Wiki should therefore update the value without erasing the context that it belonged to an older build.
