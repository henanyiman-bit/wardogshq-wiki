## What GGX 17 is for

GGX 17 fits the **secondary weapon** part of the roster. In practical terms, that makes it most relevant to **backup fighting, close emergencies and budget-conscious loadouts**. That role description is more useful than treating the weapon as a universal “best” choice, because WARDOGS mixes travel distance, objective pressure, vehicle threats and persistent spending in the same life.

## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$9,800** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.

- **Ammunition:** 9x19mm. Use the live vendor and current attachment UI as the final compatibility authority.
- **Observed damage:** 22. Do not convert one base number into a guaranteed armored or ranged TTK without controlled same-build testing.
- **Effective range:** 50 m. Treat this as a catalogue reference, not a promise that every fight at that distance is efficient.
- **Rate of fire:** 600 RPM. Higher rate changes ammunition use and follow-up speed as well as theoretical output.
