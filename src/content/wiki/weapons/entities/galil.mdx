## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$7,800** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.

## Combat Role

The Galil is a heavier general-purpose rifle for players who want a 5.56x45mm primary without paying the M4's observed price. Its 400 m reference sits between compact objective rifles and longer-reaching alternatives. The weapon makes the most sense in an infantry kit that expects sustained outdoor movement with occasional building fights.

## Effective Usage

Use semi fire for distant targets and controlled automatic fire when pressure closes the gap. At 3.95 kg, the recorded weight is the clearest tradeoff: plan armor, ammunition and utility after accounting for a relatively heavy primary. The 650 RPM rate rewards accuracy and target tracking more than volume alone.

## Strengths

- $2,200 observed price leaves more budget than several premium rifles.
- 400 m reference covers common road, field-edge and objective approaches.
- Shared 5.56x45mm context makes comparison with other rifles straightforward.

## Weaknesses

- Heavier recorded weight can constrain the rest of the loadout.
- Lower fire rate than the M4 reduces raw close-range output.
- It is not a dedicated marksman solution for the longest sightlines.

## Best Scenarios

Pick the Galil for budget-conscious rifle play across villages, industrial lanes and medium open ground. It is less attractive for lightweight pilot kits, highly mobile interior clearing or builds where every kilogram is reserved for specialist equipment.
