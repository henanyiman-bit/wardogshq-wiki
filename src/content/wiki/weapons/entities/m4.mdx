That role description is more useful than treating the weapon as a universal “best” choice, because WARDOGS mixes travel distance, objective pressure, vehicle threats and persistent spending in the same life.

## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$7,200** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.

## Combat Role

The M4 is the premium flexible rifle in this comparison set. Its observed 500 m range, 800 RPM and low 2.92 kg weight support a player who must move quickly while remaining useful across open approaches and objective interiors. That versatility comes with a $2,800 purchase price that should be justified by the planned life.

## Effective Usage

Use semi fire or disciplined bursts when crossing broad terrain, then reserve automatic fire for decisive close pressure. The low recorded weight leaves room for protection, medical gear or additional ammunition, but the rifle's high rate can consume that ammunition quickly. A player who survives and changes positions can extract more value than one who treats it as a disposable entry weapon.

## Strengths

- 500 m effective-range reference supports broad mixed-terrain use.
- 800 RPM provides fast follow-up fire when a fight becomes close.
- 2.92 kg recorded weight is favorable for a full-featured rifle.

## Weaknesses

- $2,800 is a meaningful persistent-cash commitment.
- High automatic rate can waste ammunition during uncertain contacts.
- Versatility does not replace specialist launchers or true long-range tools.

## Best Scenarios

The M4 fits experienced general infantry, mobile medics and objective players expecting several types of engagement in one life. Avoid it when a cheap rifle would solve the same task or when the budget must prioritize a vehicle or specialist system.
