## What Super-45 is for

Super-45 fits the **close-range automatic weapon** part of the roster. In practical terms, that makes it most relevant to **interiors, vehicle crews, dense objective fights and fast movement**. That role description is more useful than treating the weapon as a universal “best” choice, because WARDOGS mixes travel distance, objective pressure, vehicle threats and persistent spending in the same life.

## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$7,400** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.

## Ammo, range and fire-rate context

- **Ammunition:** .45 ACP. Use the live vendor and current attachment UI as the final compatibility authority.
- **Observed damage:** 30. Do not convert one base number into a guaranteed armored or ranged TTK without controlled same-build testing.
- **Effective range:** 50 m. Treat this as a catalogue reference, not a promise that every fight at that distance is efficient.
- **Rate of fire:** 1200 RPM. Higher rate changes ammunition use and follow-up speed as well as theoretical output.
- **Weight:** 3 kg. When recorded, weight belongs in the full kit decision rather than being read in isolation.
