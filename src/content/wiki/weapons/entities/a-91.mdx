## Combat Role

The A-91 is best treated as a compact assault-rifle option for players moving between streets, compounds and short open crossings. Its observed 300 m range places the emphasis on controlled mid-range work rather than prolonged fire across the widest terrain. Use it when the route demands rifle damage but frequent direction changes make a long-range posture impractical.

## Effective Usage

Advance from cover to cover and take short engagements where the target can be identified before committing ammunition. The 700 RPM reference supports deliberate bursts instead of indiscriminate automatic fire. Because ammunition and price are not recorded in this snapshot, verify both at the live vendor before building the rest of the kit around it.

## Strengths

- Assault-rifle damage profile in a practical 300 m envelope.
- Moderate rate of fire that supports measured follow-up shots.
- Flexible for objective routes that alternate between buildings and exterior lanes.

## Weaknesses

- Public data does not currently establish ammunition, weight or purchase price.
- The shorter recorded range gives less margin on exposed long approaches.
- Its role overlaps several rifles, so live handling should decide the final choice.

## Best Scenarios

Choose the A-91 for mixed urban pushes, factory approaches and squads expecting repeated medium-distance contacts. Skip it when the plan centers on long overwatch, extreme close-quarters speed or a specialist anti-vehicle task.
