## What T-21 is for

T-21 fits the **general-purpose infantry rifle** part of the roster. In practical terms, that makes it most relevant to **mid-range objective pushes, mixed terrain and flexible infantry work**. That role description is more useful than treating the weapon as a universal “best” choice, because WARDOGS mixes travel distance, objective pressure, vehicle threats and persistent spending in the same life.

## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$9,400** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.

## Ammo, range and fire-rate context

- **Ammunition:** 5.56x45mm. Use the live vendor and current attachment UI as the final compatibility authority.
- **Observed damage:** 28. Do not convert one base number into a guaranteed armored or ranged TTK without controlled same-build testing.
- **Effective range:** 550 m. Treat this as a catalogue reference, not a promise that every fight at that distance is efficient.
- **Rate of fire:** 750 RPM. Higher rate changes ammunition use and follow-up speed as well as theoretical output.
- **Weight:** 3.27 kg. When recorded, weight belongs in the full kit decision rather than being read in isolation.

## Combat Role

The T-21 is the economy-focused long-reach rifle in the observed assault-rifle set. A $600 price paired with a 550 m reference makes it relevant to players protecting their bankroll while still preparing for open terrain. It should be evaluated as a value tool rather than assumed to outperform more expensive rifles in every handling category.

## Effective Usage

Take advantage of range by firing from stable cover and moving before enemies can fix the position. The 750 RPM rate provides useful follow-up speed, but controlled bursts preserve ammunition and make the inexpensive purchase work across a longer life. Its 3.27 kg weight is manageable, leaving budget—not necessarily capacity—as the main reason to choose it.

## Strengths

- Very low $600 observed purchase price.
- 550 m reference is the longest among the compared general rifles here.
- Semi and automatic modes support transitions between open and built terrain.

## Weaknesses

- Low price alone does not prove superior recoil or handling.
- A long catalogue range is not a guarantee of accuracy in every stance.
- Players may overspend on attachments and erase its economy advantage.

## Best Scenarios

Use the T-21 for budget recovery, low-risk scouting and infantry routes with long approaches. It is especially useful when the team needs capable rifles without consuming cash reserved for transport, armor or support equipment.
