## What Compound Bow is for

Compound Bow fits the **specialist projectile weapon** part of the roster. In practical terms, that makes it most relevant to **niche play where deliberate shot timing matters more than volume of fire**. That role description is more useful than treating the weapon as a universal “best” choice, because WARDOGS mixes travel distance, objective pressure, vehicle threats and persistent spending in the same life.

## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$9,200** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.
