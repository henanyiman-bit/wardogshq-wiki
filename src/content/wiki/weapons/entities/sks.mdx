## What SKS is for

SKS fits the **precision semi-automatic rifle** part of the roster. In practical terms, that makes it most relevant to **medium-to-long lanes where follow-up shots matter**. That role description is more useful than treating the weapon as a universal “best” choice, because WARDOGS mixes travel distance, objective pressure, vehicle threats and persistent spending in the same life.

## Economy check

From the $10,000 starting balance, the observed buy-in leaves **$7,600** before ammunition, protection, medicine and attachments.

The budget question matters because WARDOGS cash persists between matches. A weapon purchase is not isolated from the rest of the loadout: ammunition, armor, medicine, backpack space and attachments all compete for the same balance.

## Ammo, range and fire-rate context

- **Ammunition:** 7.62x39mm. Use the live vendor and current attachment UI as the final compatibility authority.
- **Observed damage:** 42. Do not convert one base number into a guaranteed armored or ranged TTK without controlled same-build testing.
- **Effective range:** 400 m. Treat this as a catalogue reference, not a promise that every fight at that distance is efficient.
- **Rate of fire:** 420 RPM. Higher rate changes ammunition use and follow-up speed as well as theoretical output.
- **Weight:** 3.85 kg. When recorded, weight belongs in the full kit decision rather than being read in isolation.
