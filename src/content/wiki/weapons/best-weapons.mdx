A defensible “best weapons” answer needs a complete current comparison dataset. That dataset is not available in the verified Season 1 / Patch 0.11 research snapshot, so this page does **not** invent a tier list.

> **Scope note:** Official material confirms several weapon-related entities but does not publish the full damage, recoil, range, price and unlock matrix required for a trustworthy ranking.

## What is currently verified

Official Season 1 material names the **Deagle**, the **PP-19 50-round drum magazine**, **7.62x54mm AP** ammunition and **5.56mm AP** ammunition. These prove the relevant weapon/attachment/ammo systems exist, but they are not enough to rank the whole arsenal.

## How to choose without a fake tier list

Match the weapon to the expected range and role, then account for the persistent-cash cost of replacing the full kit. Medic, Support, Driver and Pilot lives can value different equipment because the weapon is only one part of the task.

## What this page still needs

A future evidence-backed ranking requires version-bound weapon stats, current vendor prices, attachment effects and unlock requirements. Until those records are captured, any numbered “top weapon” list would be speculative.

## A better comparison framework

Use four questions before calling a weapon better for a specific job:

1. **What range dominates the task?** Interior pushes and open approaches create different needs.
2. **What does the weapon cost inside the whole loadout?** A rifle that consumes the budget needed for armor, medicine or utility can be the wrong purchase.
3. **What ammunition and part relationships are actually captured?** Missing compatibility should stay unknown.
4. **Are the compared numbers from the same build?** Mixing old damage with new RPM can create a fake conclusion.

## What this page will not do

It will not turn one stat into a universal winner. Recoil, accuracy, reload, armor interaction, hit location and current-build handling all matter before a true performance ranking can be defended.
