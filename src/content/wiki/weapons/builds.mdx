Weapon builds are primarily an **attachment and ammunition configuration** question. The current research confirms those systems exist, but it does not contain a complete attachment-effect table, so this guide stays focused on build methodology rather than fabricated recoil or damage claims.

> **Scope note:** Season 1 / Patch 0.11 sources name specific attachments and AP ammunition, but do not provide enough normalized values to publish a universal build recipe for every gun.

## Start with the weapon's job

Choose the engagement role first: close-range pressure, general infantry use, or a more specialized task. Add attachments only when their verified effect supports that job; do not assume an attachment is beneficial merely because it exists.

## Ammunition is part of the build

Official material names **7.62x54mm AP** and **5.56mm AP** ammunition. That establishes AP ammunition as a real build consideration, but this snapshot does not claim unsupported penetration values or damage multipliers.

## Build data still required

A full build database needs current attachment effects, compatible-weapon relationships, prices and unlock requirements. Those fields should enter the SSOT before this page publishes per-weapon presets.

## Build around the route

A useful weapon build begins with where the next life is going. A Control Zone interior push, long valley approach, vehicle-defense role and FOB support route can justify very different weapon and utility choices.

## Build budget checklist

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Primary | Does the weapon fit the expected distance? |
| Ammunition | Is the correct ammo relationship confirmed? |
| Parts | Are the attachments actually captured as compatible? |
| Protection | Did the weapon purchase crowd out survivability? |
| Utility | Does the kit still perform the role you picked? |

Treat attachment trees and exact performance as version-sensitive unless they are captured for the current build.
