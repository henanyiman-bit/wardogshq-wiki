## How the money loop connects

<div class="mechanic-flow"><div class="mechanic-step"><strong>1 · Buy</strong>Choose the kit or vehicle for a real task</div><div class="mechanic-step"><strong>2 · Deploy</strong>Reach useful team activity</div><div class="mechanic-step"><strong>3 · Earn</strong>Revive, transport or control</div><div class="mechanic-step"><strong>4 · Persist</strong>Carry the result forward</div></div>

<div class="wiki-data-split">
<div>
### Officially confirmed earning routes

| Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Reviving squadmates | Keeps friendly presence alive while earning cash |
| Transporting friendlies | Converts vehicle use into team and economy value |
| Controlling the objective | Links the win condition directly to money |
| Hot Zone activity | Adds a double-cash opportunity, with higher risk |
</div>
<div>
### Spending checklist
- What is my job this life?
- How much of the bankroll am I risking?
- Can this purchase produce team value quickly?
- Can I afford the rebuy if this life ends early?
- Would a cheaper tool solve the same problem?
</div>
