## Where the Drill Rig fits

WARDOGS officially supports building, forward operating bases, logistics and a Hot Zone cash layer. Current community explanations place the Drill Rig at the intersection of those systems: a team establishes a forward position, supplies the relevant structure and uses the Drill as part of an attempt to influence high-value map pressure.

That makes “Drills” less like a collectible and more like an operational objective. The useful question is not simply where a Drill exists, but whether your team has the construction, supply and map position needed to make the effort worthwhile.

## FOB connection

The Drill Rig is commonly discussed in the context of a **Forward Operating Base**. Official Season 1 balance material confirms that FOBs are a real economy choice and lists a vendor price of **$7,500** in that snapshot. The same changelog confirms the Large Hammer as a Support-track item and moved its unlock to **Support level 8**.

Those official facts establish that building has real cost and progression context. The [FOB guide](/building/fob/) owns the broader construction workflow.

## Supply connection

A Drill-oriented play naturally depends on logistics because a forward structure is only useful if the team can support the position. WARDOGS' large map gives vehicles and transport enough space to matter, so supply movement can become a real team task rather than background flavor.

The current official source set does not publish a complete Drill-specific supply table. This Wiki therefore avoids exact “bring X of item Y” instructions unless a future verified source establishes them.

## Hot Zone connection

Current player reports describe Drill Rigs as another way to affect Hot Zone positioning. That is strategically important because Team17 describes the Hot Zone as a double-cash opportunity. A successful Drill play can therefore be interpreted as an attempt to move high-value activity toward a location your team is prepared to use.

This is also why a Drill can be a poor investment when the team has no follow-up. Construction, supply and protection all consume attention. The mechanic only creates value when the resulting position supports the objective or an economic opportunity.

## Drills versus Towers

[Towers](/map/towers/) are associated in current community reporting with code fragments and a completed code. Drills are associated more closely with FOB and logistics play. Both are discussed in relation to Hot Zone influence, but the route to that influence is different.

Keeping those mechanics separate matters for a Wiki because otherwise a reader can easily confuse two different tasks and follow the wrong preparation path.

## Practical checklist

Before committing to a Drill-related plan, ask:

- Is the forward position useful relative to the current Control Zone?
- Can the team move supplies to it without abandoning the objective?
- Is someone actually prepared to protect or use the position?
- Would a vehicle or direct objective push create more immediate value?
- Is the current patch still behaving the way the guide you are reading describes?
