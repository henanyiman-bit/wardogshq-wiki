## Build the life around the job

Because the economy is persistent, the cost of the vehicle or kit is part of the strategy. A role that repeatedly completes useful transport or support tasks can be economically sensible even when it is not producing the highest kill count.

## Combine earnings with objective value

The Control Zone is the match objective, so transport and logistics are most valuable when they shorten the path between team resources and the active fight. Current community reports also describe substantial earnings from logistics, supply delivery and FOB support, but exact payout numbers are not standardized here as official facts.

## Avoid fake payout calculators

This snapshot does not contain a verified universal per-passenger, per-supply or per-minute payout table. Any guide that presents exact values without version-specific evidence should be treated cautiously.
