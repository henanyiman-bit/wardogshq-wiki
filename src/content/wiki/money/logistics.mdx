WARDOGS can reward a logistics player for **useful team activity instead of kills alone**. Official publisher material explicitly includes transporting friendlies among cash-generating team actions, while the building layer creates continuing demand for moving supplies toward forward positions.

## Supply runs

A supply run is valuable when it connects a real team need to a forward position: move material toward a FOB or active objective area, avoid wasting an expensive vehicle, and combine the delivery with passenger transport when the route allows it.

Current community guides describe multiple supply categories and different vehicle capacities, but exact pallet values and capacities are not promoted into this Wiki's SSOT until they can be resolved against current official data.

## Transport income

The reliable principle is simple: WARDOGS rewards useful transport. Do not assume an old beta per-passenger figure is current unless the live game or an official update confirms it.

## Connect logistics to the objective

The best logistics route is not necessarily the longest route or the one with the most cargo. The Control Zone, Hot Zone, FOB position and current team needs determine whether moving players, construction supplies or other support resources has useful match value.
