**Yes. Cash persists from match to match in WARDOGS.** Team17 describes this persistence as a core metagame: players begin with $10,000, buy a loadout each life, and carry their remaining cash forward.

## What persistence does mean

Your balance is not simply reset at the end of every match. This is why the cost of a weapon, equipment choice or vehicle matters beyond the current round.

## What this page does not assume

The current official sources used for this snapshot do not define every edge case players search for, such as every disconnect, early-leave, refund or server-transfer scenario. Those cases are omitted rather than turned into guessed rules.

## Practical implication

Treat every respawn as a budget decision. Expensive equipment can be justified when it supports a high-value role or objective push, but the persistent economy rewards maintaining enough cash to keep contributing across future lives and matches.
