## Terrain and objective are different layers

<div class="wiki-data-split">
<div>
### Battlefield layer
The full terrain determines roads, cover, water crossings, hills, built-up areas and vehicle access. It tells you **how** you can move.

### Objective layer
The randomized Control Zone determines **where** player presence matters for scoring in the current match. A familiar route can become irrelevant if it no longer serves the zone.
</div>
<div>
### Read the map in this order
1. Confirm the map name.
2. Find the current Control Zone.
3. Identify nearby tower / support systems.
4. Choose infantry or vehicle approach.
5. Re-check the route after the fight shifts.
</div>
</div>
